CONTROLLER MODES

One Phone. Five Controllers.

Choose your playstyle. Switch modes instantly. No drivers, no lag.

🎮

Gamepad

Transform your phone into a traditional game controller with full button mapping.

  • Dual analog sticks
  • D-pad + ABXY buttons
  • L1/R1/L2/R2 triggers
  • Haptic vibration
  • Button remapping
🏎️

Racing Wheel

Tilt your phone left and right for gyroscope-based steering with full pedal controls.

  • Gyroscope steering
  • Throttle & brake pedals
  • Handbrake + gear shift
  • Auto-centering
  • Sensitivity tuning
🌀

Gyroscope

Use physical phone movement as a precision input device for aiming and motion control.

  • 3-axis motion control
  • Tilt-to-aim
  • Adjustable sensitivity
  • Axis inversion
  • Zero-point calibration
🖱️

Mouse

Turn your phone into a wireless trackpad with full click and scroll support.

  • Precision touchpad
  • Left / right / middle click
  • Two-finger scrolling
  • Drag and drop
  • DPI control
⌨️

Keyboard

Use your phone as a wireless keyboard with WASD, function keys, and custom shortcuts.

  • Full QWERTY layout
  • WASD gaming mode
  • Function key row
  • Custom shortcuts
  • Key remapping
🎛️

Custom Builder

Drag, drop, and resize controls anywhere on your screen. Save unlimited profiles.

  • Drag-and-drop editor
  • Add any control type
  • Save & load profiles
  • Share to community
  • Import game presets
